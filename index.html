ZEDFRAME

Windows screen recording and local video editing.

ZEDFRAME combines screen recording, a timeline editor, zoom and cursor effects, video backgrounds, local captions, manual privacy blur, on-demand text scanning for suggested privacy masks, proxy previews, project recovery, and sequential exports. You can record a new video, import an existing video, or reopen a saved project before editing.

Recording and editing work locally without signing in. Optional Google sign-in identifies your ZEDFRAME account through Supabase; it does not upload your recordings.
